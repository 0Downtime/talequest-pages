TaleQuest Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-06-07

TaleQuest is a private family scavenger hunt app for children ages 4-8, used with a parent or caregiver.

This policy describes TaleQuest privacy practices for TestFlight and App Store review.

Data We Collect

  • Child nickname and buddy/avatar choice.
  • Hunt title and general place label typed by the grown-up, such as "Backyard" or "Inside".
  • Mission progress, mission status, QR scan answers, and timestamps.
  • Proof photos and sound recordings submitted for grown-up review.
  • iCloud records needed for parent-controlled private proof sync when the adult host uses iCloud.
  • Local parent permission record for this prototype.
  • Crash and diagnostic data, such as crash logs, app version, build number, device/OS details, and reliability logs, collected through Firebase Crashlytics to find and fix app problems.

Data We Do Not Collect

  • Child email.
  • Child full name.
  • Precise GPS location.
  • Public profile photos, public posts, or public sharing.
  • Open chat, friend lists, or social graph.
  • Advertising identifiers, tracking data, or behavioral analytics data.
  • Child-facing purchases.

How Data Is Used

Data is used only to run a private family hunt, let a grown-up review proof, show a family recap, sync proof to the adult host's private iCloud database when available, and diagnose crashes or reliability problems.

Parent Permission

A grown-up must review the child privacy notice before the app asks for camera, microphone, join, proof, progress, or recap data collection.

The current app stores a local prototype permission record. Public launch requires counsel-approved verifiable parental consent.

Sharing

TaleQuest does not sell child data and does not use child data for ads, tracking, behavioral profiling, or behavioral analytics.

Private iCloud proof sync uses Apple's CloudKit infrastructure for app functionality. Remote multiplayer shared rooms are paused until stronger child-data isolation exists.

Crash diagnostics use Firebase Crashlytics for app reliability. Crashlytics is not used for ads, tracking, or behavioral profiling.

Parent Rights

  • Review child data in Privacy Controls and Recap.
  • Delete individual proof submissions.
  • Delete all hunt data.
  • Revoke permission and stop future child data collection.
  • Request deletion of data that remains in private iCloud proof sync.

Retention

Proof media is event-limited and can be deleted by a grown-up at any time. TaleQuest is configured to remove proof media after 30 days during app cleanup and when syncing private iCloud proof records. Child personal information is not retained indefinitely.

Security

TaleQuest maintains child-data safeguards, limits access to child data, and requires providers that maintain child data to protect confidentiality, security, and integrity.
