TaleQuest Support

TaleQuest is a private family scavenger hunt app for children ages 4-8, used with a parent or caregiver.

This page lists support contacts and beta testing guidance for TaleQuest.

Contact

Support email: support@christopherbrien.com

Privacy email: privacy@christopherbrien.com

We aim to respond to beta support messages within 3 business days.

External TestFlight Focus

  • Start a private local family hunt.
  • Join kid play with a nickname and buddy/avatar.
  • Complete photo, sound, QR, and choice missions.
  • Approve proof as the grown-up and review the recap.
  • Use Privacy Controls to delete proof, delete hunt data, or revoke permission.

Known Beta Limits

  • Remote multiplayer shared rooms are paused for child-data isolation.
  • Private iCloud proof sync is for the adult host when iCloud is available.
  • The parent permission record is stored locally for this prototype.
